# Numerical Analysis of the Airy Equation in the WKB Approximation

[Course Report (PDF)](https://serein-xi.github.io/assets/uploads/airy-project.pdf)

## Overview

This computational physics project studies numerical solutions of the Airy equation in the context of the Wentzel–Kramers–Brillouin (WKB) approximation near a classical turning point.

## Physics Motivation

The standard WKB approximation breaks down where the local semiclassical wave number vanishes. The Airy equation provides a local model for connecting oscillatory and exponentially varying solutions near a turning point.

## Methodology

- Study numerical integration of the Airy differential equation.
- Use analytic Airy functions as reference solutions.
- Compare numerical accuracy and stability under different integration strategies.
- Examine how contamination by a growing solution can affect a numerically computed decaying solution.

## Discussion

The problem illustrates how boundary conditions and integration direction can determine whether a numerical solution is physically meaningful, even when the underlying differential equation is known exactly.
