# Projects

A selection of my research and computational physics projects, ranging from experimental particle physics to statistical and semiclassical physics.

## [Solar-pp Analysis: ACE Efficiency & Cut Acceptance](/projects/solar-pp.html)

Waveform-level simulation and signal injection to study reconstruction and selection efficiency for solar pp neutrinos.

## [XENONnT Detector Calibration & Energy Resolution](/projects/xenonnt-calibration.html)

Cross-run detector response, light and charge gains, energy reconstruction, and resolution.

## [Probing Metastability and Free-Energy Barriers with Discrete Diffusion Models](/projects/discrete-diffusion.html)

Discrete generative modeling of phase coexistence and metastability in the Potts model.

## [Machine-Learning Detection of Potts-Model Phase Transitions](/projects/potts-phase-transitions.html)

Machine-learning diagnostics of phase transitions, finite-size effects, and parameter sensitivity.

## [Numerical Analysis of the Airy Equation in the WKB Approximation](/projects/airy-wkb.html)

Numerical stability and accuracy near a semiclassical turning point.

Further details, figures, and public research materials are available on the individual project pages as they are prepared. Collaboration-restricted results are not included here.
