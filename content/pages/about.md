# About Me

## Background

I am Zixi Chen, an undergraduate student in Physics at *The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen* (CUHK-Shenzhen).

My primary academic interest is experimental particle physics. In particular, I am interested in what can be learned about fundamental particles through low-background detectors, precise calibration, and careful modeling of detector response.

## Research Experience

### XENONnT Analysis

My work includes studies of light and charge response, energy calibration, and signal reconstruction and analysis-cut efficiencies. I use simulation and data-analysis tools to investigate how detector effects influence physics measurements.

### Computational Physics

I have also explored numerical and machine-learning approaches to statistical physics, including sampling and phase-transition studies in Potts models.

## Technical Interests

- Scientific Python and reproducible data analysis
- Detector simulation and Monte Carlo techniques
- Statistical fitting and uncertainty estimation
- Signal reconstruction and waveform-level diagnostics
- High-performance computing workflows

## Academic Interests

My longer-term interests include dark matter detection, neutrino physics, rare-event experiments, and the development of robust analysis methods for large experimental collaborations.
